Key Projects

📸 AI Face Attendance (AIFace) Edge AI & Computer Vision

View Repository

An enterprise-grade, 100% offline biometric facial recognition attendance system for Android & iOS. Built with Flutter, Google ML Kit, and TensorFlow Lite (MobileFaceNet). Features high-speed face detection (~18ms), 192-dimensional embedding extraction, and multi-vector anti-spoofing liveness verification.

Anti-Spoofing: Defends against photo prints & screen attacks via eye-blink and texture classification.
On-Device Inference: Runs locally with zero cloud latency and total privacy compliance.
BLoC Architecture: Predictable, reactive state management separating business logic from UI.
High Precision: Cosine distance matching with calibrated threshold for low false matches (<0.1%).
